Elsewhere: LinkedIn · GitHub · Blog

Who should get in touch

  • Hosting providers and smaller cloud operators who need storage without a large operations team.
  • Server OEMs and integrators who want one storage stack on every box they ship.
  • Enterprises whose data has to stay on premises or under their own control.
  • Technology partners whose product could build on FRAD.

What helps the first reply

  • Your hardware and the number of nodes.
  • The workload: block, file, S3, or a mix.
  • Connected to the internet, or air-gapped.
  • Your timeline, and what would make the evaluation a success for you.

What happens next

  • A reply within one working day.
  • A call to go through your requirements.
  • A technical evaluation: on your own machine in a browser first, then a pilot on your hardware.
